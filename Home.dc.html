Kin + Inu

Kinship, plus "inu" — the Japanese word for dog.

Kinu Supply is the merch label of Studio Kinu, a Singapore creative studio run by pet people. Every breed is drawn from real-life observation — the zoomies, the side-eye, the very serious nap schedules — then printed on things worth keeping.

Our pieces are badges of honour for those who proudly declare their companions family. You'll find us at pop-ups around the island, or at the studio on Tannery Lane.
